Refund Policy

Effective Date: October 14, 2026

1. Introduction

At Crazy Cravings, we aim to provide a satisfying dining and ordering experience. This Refund Policy explains how refund and cancellation requests are handled.

2. Order Confirmation

Please review your order details, items, quantities, address, and payment amount before confirming your order. Contact us promptly if you notice an error.

3. Order Cancellation

Cancellation requests should be made as soon as possible. Once food preparation or dispatch has started, cancellation may not be possible except where required by law or our policy.

4. Eligible Refunds

A refund may be considered for duplicate payments, confirmed failed orders that were charged, or other eligible issues verified by our team.

5. Missing or Incorrect Items

If an item is missing or differs from your confirmed order, contact us promptly and provide your order details so we can investigate and determine an appropriate resolution.

6. Food Quality Concerns

If you have a concern about food quality or safety, stop consuming the affected item and contact us promptly with your order details and a description of the issue.

7. Duplicate or Failed Payments

If you were charged more than once or payment was deducted for an unconfirmed order, contact us with the transaction reference so the payment can be checked.

8. Refund Processing Time

Once a refund is approved, processing time may depend on the payment provider or bank. We will communicate any available refund status or reference details.

9. Refund Method

Approved refunds will generally be returned through the original payment method where supported. Additional verification may be required in certain cases.

10. Restaurant Reservations

Reservation deposits or advance payments, if applicable, are subject to the cancellation conditions communicated when the booking is made and applicable law.

11. How to Request a Refund

Contact our team with your order number, registered contact details, payment reference if relevant, and a clear description of the issue.

12. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when our services or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised date.

13. Contact Information

For refund questions or help with an order, contact Crazy Cravings through the contact information provided on our website. Please keep your order reference available.
